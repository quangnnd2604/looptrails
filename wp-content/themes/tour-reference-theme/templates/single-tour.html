Tour Overview

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Ha Giang City → Quan Ba Heaven Gate → Yen Minh

Depart morning from Ha Giang town. Climb up Bac Sum Pass, admire Quan Ba Twin Mountains, and ride through pine forests to Yen Minh town for local homestay check-in and family dinner.

Day 2

Yen Minh → Tham Ma Pass → Vuong Palace → Dong Van

Conquer the famous 9-turn Tham Ma pass, visit the 100-year-old H’mong King Palace, and explore the Dong Van ancient street at night with local street food.

Day 3

Dong Van → Ma Pi Leng Pass → Tu San Canyon → Du Gia

Ride across the legendary Ma Pi Leng Pass, take a boat tour on the emerald Nho Que River through Tu San Canyon, and continue along scenic backroads to Du Gia waterfall village.
